Risk-register spikes

Half a day each. Run them on the actual iPhone, installed to the home screen.

Gate: nothing else is worth building until 1, 2 and 4 pass. If spike 1 fails, the audio half of the project fails and a native wrapper is back on the table.
  1. 1 · Background audio & lock screen

    One audio element, one MP3, Media Session. Install to home screen, start playback, lock the phone. Does it keep playing for 10 minutes? Do the lock screen controls appear and work?

  2. 2 · Storage quota

    Write 500 MB of blobs to IndexedDB in a chunked loop, check estimate(), then reboot the phone and verify the bytes are still there.

  3. 3 · foliate-js on iOS Safari

    Render an EPUB, paginate, change font size, verify a CFI round-trips through a reload.

  4. 4 · Service worker range serving

    Serve a large MP3 from IndexedDB through the SW. Verify 206 responses and that seeking to 90% is instant rather than downloading the whole file first.

  5. 5 · Screen Time / content filter

    Confirm this domain loads under your existing restrictions.
