Spike 2 — Storage quota

Procedure
  1. Install to the home screen and open from there.
  2. Press Request persistent, then Write 500 MB.
  3. Note the reported quota. A single audiobook is 100–400 MB.
  4. Reboot the phone, reopen, press Verify.

Pass: 500 MB writes without error, and every chunk survives a reboot.
