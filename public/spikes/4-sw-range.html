Spike 4 — Service worker range serving

Procedure
  1. Register SW, then Download & store (~30 MB LibriVox track).
  2. Run range tests — checks 206 status, Content-Range and Content-Length.
  3. Attach to audio, then drag the scrubber to 90%.

Pass: every range test returns 206 with the right byte count, and seeking to 90% starts playing in well under a second — no full-file download first.
